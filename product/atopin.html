Atopin

LivestockOrnamental BirdsPetsPre-Anasethetic

A veterinary pharmaceutical product by Symans Pharmaceuticals (Private) Limited. The information below is preserved exactly from the original product record.

Composition
Each ml contains: Atropine sulphate 1mg
Indication
Atopin Injection is used as a preanesthetic adjuvant or to reduce salivation, bronchial secretion or internal peristalsis associated with colic or diarrhea. As an antidote for parasympathomimetic drugs.
Dosage
S.C/I.M and slow I.V Injection Fancy Birds: 0.5ml per 10kg b/wt. SC. Cattle, Horses, Camel, Foals, Calves, Sheep and Goat: 1ml per 25kg b/w. I.M for 3 to 5 days. Dogs & Cats: 0.5ml per 10kg b/wt. Intra-Uterine Route: Acute / Chronic metritis in mares. Cows & Bualoes: 20ml Gentasym 10% Solution in 200ml Distilled water of N.Saline twice with 24 hours intervals
Packing
10 ml, 50 ml, 100 ml.
Professional reference only: Product information on this page is provided for professional reference and should not replace the advice of a qualified veterinarian. Always follow the product label and veterinary guidance for dosage and use.
