Cholipol Super

Poultry

A veterinary pharmaceutical product by Symans Pharmaceuticals (Private) Limited. The information below is preserved exactly from the original product record.

Composition
Each kg contains: Chloline Chloride 450gm DL-Methionine 40gm L-Lysine Monohyd 40gm Manganese Sulphate 90gm Zinc Sulphate 22gm Vitamin B2-80 3gm Vitamin D3 5gm Di-Cal-Phosphate 1kg
Indication
Lameness, Dermatitis, Defeathering, Myopathy, Exudative Diathesis and perosis. Actively enhances growth, production & fertiliy.
Dosage
Layers, breeders and fancy birds: 25 to 50 gm per 50 kg feed Broilers: 50 to 100 gm per 50 kg feed
Packing
1kg, 5kg, 25kg
